# Plateful

**Make something delicious with what you already have.**

Plateful is a hackathon project by **Byte & Bite** that helps people decide what to make for dinner. Scan your fridge for ingredients, find recipes from around the web, and keep favorite ideas and shopping needs together in a cozy, pixel-art kitchen.

**Live demo:** [Try Plateful](https://plateful-final.onrender.com/index.html)

## What you can do

- **Scan your fridge:** Upload or take a photo of your fridge. AI suggests the ingredients it can identify, and you can review the list.
- **Scan with your phone:** Open the scanner using a QR code and send the results back to your laptop.
- **Find recipes:** Search for a dish, such as chocolate chip cookies, and browse links to recipes on their original websites.
- **Save favorites:** Keep recipe ideas in your recipe book and collect items for your next grocery trip.
- **Explore the kitchen:** Move through a playful, pixel-art experience built around the fridge and recipe book.

## Built with

- HTML, CSS, and JavaScript
- Node.js
- Gemini API for fridge image recognition and ingredient-based recipe ideas
- Brave Search API for web recipe results
- Render for hosting
